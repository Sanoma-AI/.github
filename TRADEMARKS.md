# Trademark Policy

"Sanoma" is used by Sanoma-AI as the name of its business operations software. US trademark clearance is pending, and this policy will be finalized before launch.

## Allowed

- Describing compatibility, for example "works with Sanoma", for a connector, module or service that integrates with Sanoma.
- Referring to the Sanoma project accurately in articles, talks and documentation.

## Not allowed

- Naming a product or hosted service in a way that suggests it is Sanoma's own offering, for example "Sanoma Cloud".
- Using the Sanoma name or logo in a way that implies endorsement by Sanoma-AI without permission.

Community connectors should be named `sanoma-connector-<vendor>`.

Questions about use of the name: open an issue in this repo until a contact address is published.
